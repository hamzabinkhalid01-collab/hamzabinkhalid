About me

4 years. 50+ clients. Zero missed deadlines.

I'm Hamza, a graphic and social media designer based in Lahore, Pakistan. Over the last 4+ years I've worked with more than 50 clients — Lahore-based fashion labels, construction firms, tech brands, and international clients in the UK and USA.

My core work is social media design and print: feed content, campaigns, billboards, standees, and company profiles. I also integrate AI image generation tools into commercial workflows — producing studio-quality visuals efficiently, a skill most designers still don't offer.

I'm detail-oriented, responsive, and used to managing multiple clients simultaneously without quality slipping. In-house, agency, hybrid, or remote — I'm ready.
